    About us

    A crêperie with a sweet tooth.

    Crepe Beirut makes crepes the classic way: thin batter poured on a hot plate, spread flat with the wooden rake and folded around your filling. Choose from fourteen crepes, from a simple Nutella or Lotus to Dubai, Pistachio and Triple Chocolate, then pile on extras.

    Not in a crepe mood? Order mini pancakes by the box of 12 or 25, waffles, cheesecake and tiramisu, or something cold: iced cups, shakes, frappes, smoothies, matcha and mojitos.
